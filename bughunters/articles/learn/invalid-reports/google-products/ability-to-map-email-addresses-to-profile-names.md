When you sign up for a Google account and create a profile, your profile name is
displayed in multiple locations and we generally allow other users who already
know your email address or phone number to view your profile name. This approach
is explained in our
[Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/), which as of
the latest revision of this article reads:

*If other users already have your email address or other information that
identifies you, we may show them your publicly visible Google Account
information, such as your name and photo.*

## Conclusion

If you report locations that allow you to map email addresses or phone numbers
to public profile information, we won't file a bug based on your report, as we
don't judge this to be a security vulnerability.

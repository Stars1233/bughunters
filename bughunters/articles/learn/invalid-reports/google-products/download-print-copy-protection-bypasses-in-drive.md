When sharing files in Google Drive, you can select to
"[Disable options to download, print, and copy](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2494893)".
This feature is designed to discourage others from downloading, printing, or
copying your content – but we don't consider it to be a security measure.

## Conclusion

Due to the nature of the product, the file needs to be downloaded by the browser
to render it, and there are multiple ways to access that content at a later
point. As such, we are aware that some users may be able to bypass the download
/ print / copy protection in Drive. However, we don't consider this kind of
bypass to be a security vulnerability.

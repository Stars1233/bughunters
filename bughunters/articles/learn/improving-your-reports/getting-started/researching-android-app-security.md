Getting started with security research on Android apps has an initial learning
curve which can be intimidating. This page is designed to share resources you
can access to make your learning experience as efficient as possible, with the
ultimate goal of being prepared to find vulnerabilities which you can report to
the
[Mobile VRP](/about/rules/android-friends/6618732618186752/google-mobile-vulnerability-reward-program-rules).


## Android app hacking course on Hextree

The Mobile VRP team has collaborated with two renowned security educators,
[LiveOverflow](https://www.youtube.com/liveoverflow) and
[Stacksmashing](https://www.youtube.com/stacksmashing), to create an
introductory Android hacking course. The course teaches everything from creating
your first app, to performing complex exploits.

The content is designed for anyone who is interested in security in general and
would like to focus on Android apps specifically. No prior knowledge of
security or Android app development is required!

In detail, the content is made up of:

*   12 micro-courses
*   135+ videos
*   6:30h+ of dense content
*   70+ practical hands-on challenges

For a full overview of the individual topics covered in the course, see the
[course details](https://app.hextree.io/map/android/android-continent/detail).

Interested? Sign up for free [here](https://www.hextree.io/hextree-x-google) and
start your Android app security learning adventure!

First of all, please consider if a video is truly necessary. Videos take a lot
of time to create, edit, and upload – and it takes effort on our end to watch
it, pause it at just the right moment, and squint to write down the URLs and
other pertinent details. Our vulnerability submission form is staffed by
security engineers, so, for example, there is relatively little value in putting
together a presentation about the consequences of XSS bugs.

If you feel that attaching a recording would help, or if we were so stumped that
we asked you to create one, here are some tips to make the process effective and
smooth:

*   Make sure that your video is accompanied by **written instructions** on how
    to reproduce the flaw, so that we do not have to manually jot down down the
    details from individual frames.
*   Keep the video **as short as possible** and make sure that it captures all
    the steps required to pull off the attack. Please don't record yourself
    typing a description in a Notepad window; put the text in the report
    instead :-).
*   Make sure your video has **sufficient resolution and bitrate** to make it
    possible to read the text shown on screen. Watch the uploaded video to be
    sure!
*   **Upload your video to YouTube** and set the visibility to "unlisted" (we
    can't see YouTube videos marked as "private"). Feel free to
    [add music to your video](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/94316?hl=en).

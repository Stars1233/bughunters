Thousands of people from all over the world use our vulnerability submission
form. We want to triage your report as quickly as possible. If you are not
proficient in English, using your native language can actually help us get back
to you more quickly and accurately!

While automated translators work great for many tasks, they don't necessarily
cope well with information security jargon – and when both the initial report
and the response go through a translator, it's twice as likely to be
misunderstood!

Google's Security Team has hundreds of security engineers from all over the
world, and we are pretty likely to find someone who speaks your language. Please
feel free to email us in the language you feel the most comfortable writing;
we'll do our best to email you back in the same language, too.

(And yes, Klingon is OK.)
